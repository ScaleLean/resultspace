# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: being respectful and kind, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best
for the community. Not acceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults,
harassment, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that is inappropriate
in a professional setting.

Maintainers will remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently.

## Reporting

Report incidents to **conduct@resultspace.example** (placeholder: the maintainers will set the
real address). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
